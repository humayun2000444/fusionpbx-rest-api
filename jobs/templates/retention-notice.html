How to download
Open Call Recordings
{{PORTAL_URL}}/call-recordings
  1. Sign in and open Call Recordings.
  2. Filter by date, extension or phone number.
  3. Use the download icon on a row for one recording, or tick several rows and choose Download selected for a ZIP.
Every ZIP includes the call details — who called whom, when, and for how long — so the recordings stay meaningful once they are on your own system.
Keeping a copy automatically
If you would rather not do this by hand each month, we can switch on a scheduled transfer that copies new recordings to your own server as they are made. Turn it on under Call Recordings → Downloading & backup, or reply to this email and we will set it up.
